About

I hold a BS in Information Technology from the University of Sahiwal (December 2021 – June 2025, CGPA 3.58/4.00). My work centres on applied machine learning: deep learning for image classification, natural language processing, and large language model systems built on retrieval-augmented generation, with a particular interest in medical AI.

My projects include brain MRI classification (my final year project), traffic sign recognition, a multi-agent RAG system for clinical report analysis, phishing URL detection, and tooling for single-cell RNA-seq analysis. Alongside this work I teach: I served as a Section Leader for Stanford University's Code In Place, and have taught Python, data structures and algorithms, and generative AI through Pak Angels, Iqra Fund and iCodeGuru.

I also take part in algorithmic problem-solving contests, including Meta Hacker Cup, Harvard CS50x Puzzle Day and Advent of Code.
